Design directions · v2

loqin — three actual directions

Three distinct design languages — different typography, different materials, different layouts. Same three screens (Home · Start · Running), same product decisions: one obvious lock-in action, profiles as a quick switch, manual + NFC start, essentials always reachable.

01 · Alpine 02 · Editorial 03 · Atelier
Direction 01

Alpine

Immersive photography, frosted glass, a serif display voice. The same landscape in three states — clear when you're free, veiled when starting, dark under a locked session.

PHOTOGRAPHY · GLASS · LIGHT
TypeInstrument Serif display + SF UI — editorial, calm, expensive.
MaterialFull-bleed photography under frosted glass panels.
LayoutCentered hero. One circular gesture. Everything else sits in a glass tray.
Direction 02

Editorial

Swiss-print discipline: hairlines, tabular numerals, one accent. And a signature move — the running screen inverts the whole phone to black. The device itself looks locked.

SWISS · HAIRLINES · INVERSION
TypeSpace Grotesk display + Inter — geometric, confident, engineered.
MaterialPaper white, hairline rules, one electric-blue accent.
LayoutEditorial stack: display line, quiet list, full-width action. Locked state = full black.
Direction 03

Atelier

Warm paper, engraved detail, a watch-dial timer. Craft over chrome — the app feels like a fine notebook or a mechanical watch: tactile, personal, unhurried.

ANALOG · PAPER · WARM
TypeFraunces serif with small-caps labels — stationery, stamped, human.
MaterialWarm paper with grain, hairline rules, oxblood wax-seal accents.
LayoutA seal to press, tickets for profiles, a watch dial while running.
