loqin — five ways to lock in

Same tile shape you liked, same stripped logic — pill, one gesture, no chrome, no start sheet, stop is a small ✕. What changes is the world behind it and the material of the tile: glass, solid white, solid ink, a hot gradient, and a bare outline.

1

Coast — glass

A foggy teal shoreline. The tile is frosted glass floating in the mist.

FOGGY COAST · GLASS
2

Aurora — solid white

Northern lights at night. The tile is opaque white — crisp, weighty, unmistakable.

AURORA · SOLID WHITE
3

Concrete — solid ink

Minimal architecture in soft light — the only bright direction. The tile is solid black ink on pale concrete.

ARCHITECTURE · SOLID INK
4

Ember — gradient

No photo at all — a warm abstract glow. The tile is a hot orange-to-rose gradient, the one loud thing in the room.

ABSTRACT · GRADIENT
5

Void — ghost

Deep space, near black. The tile is just an outline — a hairline rectangle floating in the void. The quietest option.

SPACE · GHOST OUTLINE
